# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

Everyone who takes part in Advanced TaskManager — users, contributors and maintainers — deserves a friendly, safe and welcoming place, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people who don't code.
- Assume good intent; ask before judging.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Credit others' ideas and work.
- Keep discussions on topic and respect people's time.
- Protect privacy: never post someone's personal details, and remove your own (user names, computer names, logs with paths) before sharing.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sharing malware, exploits aimed at other people, or ways to misuse this tool against systems you don't own or administer.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, chat — and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the contact listed in the project's home folder or repository settings. Reports are handled confidentially and promptly. People who report in good faith will not face retaliation.

## Consequences

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code, in proportion to what happened.
